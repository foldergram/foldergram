# Save a post to a collection

## Save a post

On the Home feed, hover the mouse over the "Save post" bookmark button of the first post and wait for a "Collections" popover to appear.
In the popover, click the "Create collection" plus button, type "Favorites" in the "Collection name" field and press Enter.
Verify the bookmark button of that post is now filled.

## Find it in the collection

Click "Collections" in the left menu and verify a "Favorites" collection is listed.
Open "Favorites" and verify it contains one post.
