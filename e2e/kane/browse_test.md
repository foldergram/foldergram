# Browse folders and like a post

## Home feed

Verify the Home feed shows posts from the "beach-trip" and "garden" folders and that "Likes" in the left menu shows 0.

## Open a folder

Click "Library" in the left menu and verify it shows 2 folders and 5 posts.
Click the "beach-trip" folder and verify its page shows 3 posts.

## Like a post

Click the first post in the beach-trip grid to open it in the post viewer, then click its "Like post" heart button and verify the heart turns red.
Close the post viewer with the round "×" button in the top right corner and verify the beach-trip folder page is visible again.
Click "Likes" in the left menu and verify the "Liked posts" grid shows exactly 1 photo instead of "No liked posts yet".
